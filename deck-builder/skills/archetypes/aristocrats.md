# SUB-SKILL: Aristocrats Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 25–32 (Mix of sacrifice fodder and recursive threats)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Grave-based or instant removal)
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Asymmetric wipes or death-payoff clears)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Sacrifice-for-draw engines like Village Rites, Skullclamp)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Sacrifice-synergy ramp, treasure generators, Ashtnod's Altar)
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.6 – 3.2

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Free Sacrifice Outlets (8 cards)**: Instant-speed, zero-mana sac outlets (Viscera Seer, Yahenni, Phyrexian Altar).
2. **Death-Trigger Payoffs & Drain (8 cards)**: Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, Mayhem Devil to drain opponents on creature death.
3. **Recursive Fodder & Token Generators (8 cards)**: Reassembling Skeleton, Ophiomancer, Bitterblossom, Pawn of Ulamog.
4. **Reanimation & Grave Value (8 cards)**: Mass reanimation (Living Death) and recurrable recursion engines.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **The Three-Legged Stool**: An Aristocrats engine fails if missing any one of: (1) Sac Outlet, (2) Fodder, or (3) Payoff. Keep these in equal 8-card proportions.
- **Free vs. Paid Outlets**: Prioritize free sacrifice outlets over outlets requiring mana per sac.
- **Wipe Resilience**: Board wipes often win you the game if you have drain payoffs on board; hold up sac outlets in response to wipes.
