# SUB-SKILL: Pillowfort Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 10–16 (Defensive creatures & tax enablers)
- **Targeted Removal**: 8–10
- **Board Wipes**: 4–6 (Heavy wipe suite)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10
- **Ramp**: 10–12
- **Lands**: 37–38
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.0 – 3.6

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Attack Tax & Pillowfort Enablers (8 cards)**: Propaganda, Ghostly Prison, Norn's Annex, Sphere of Safety.
2. **Damage Prevention & Redirection (8 cards)**: Deflecting Palm, Solitary Confinement, Glacial Chasm.
3. **Taxing & Stax Lite Enablers (8 cards)**: Smothering Tithe, Rhystic Study, Blind Obedience.
4. **Insurmountable Win Conditions (8 cards)**: Luminarch Ascension, Approach of the Second Sun, Test of Endurance.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Redirect Aggro**: Force opponents to attack each other while making attacking you mathematically inefficient.
- **Active Win Path**: Sitting behind taxes without a win condition leads to losing long games. Have concrete win threats.
