# SUB-SKILL: Voltron Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 6–12 (Utility & equipment support creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Asymmetric or single-target retention wipes)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Equipment/Aura-based draw, Puresteel Paladin, Sram)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Equipment ramp, Sigarda's Aid, low rocks)
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.5 – 3.2

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Equipment / Aura Package (8 cards)**: Colossus Hammer, Sword of Feast and Famine, Bear Umbra, Blackblade Reforged.
2. **Evasion & Haste Enablers (8 cards)**: Swiftfoot Boots, Lightning Greaves, Rogue's Passage, Whispersilk Cloak.
3. **Protection & Hexproof/Indestructible Grants (8 cards)**: Tamiyo's Safekeeping, Flawless Maneuver, Teferi's Protection.
4. **Free Equip / Tutor Enablers (8 cards)**: Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda's Aid, Stoneforge Mystic, Open the Armory.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Commander Damage Math**: 21 Commander Damage kills a player regardless of life total. Aim for 7+, 11+, or 21+ power thresholds.
- **Hexproof/Shroud & Protection**: Commander removal resets your entire investment. Protecting the commander is step 1.
