# SUB-SKILL: Spellslinger Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 6–12 (Magecraft & cost reduction creatures only)
- **Targeted Removal**: 8–12 (Instant/Sorcery spot removal)
- **Board Wipes**: 2–4 (Instant/Sorcery clears like Blasphemous Act, Cyclonic Rift)
- **Card Draw**: 10–12 (Cantrips, impulse draw, and spell refills)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Cost reducers + mana rocks + rituals)
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.2 – 2.8

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Cost Reducers & Rituals (8 cards)**: Goblin Electromancer, Baral, Birgi, High Tide, Jeska's Will.
2. **Magecraft & Spell Payoffs (8 cards)**: Veyran, Guttersnipe, Talrand, Young Pyromancer, Archmage Emeritus.
3. **Cantrips & Fast Velocity (8 cards)**: Ponder, Preordain, Brainstorm, Opt, Frantic Search.
4. **Spell Copying & Finishers (8 cards)**: Dualcaster Mage, Thousand-Year Storm, Crackle with Power, Bonus Round.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **High Instant/Sorcery Density**: Maintain at least 35–40 total Instants and Sorceries in the 99.
- **Cost Reduction is Key**: 1-2 cost reducers out perform standard land ramp for casting multiple spells per turn.
