# SUB-SKILL: Reanimator Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 18–24 (High-CMC win targets & enablers)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3
- **Card Draw & Looting**: 8–10 (Faithless Looting, Frantic Search, Entomb effects)
- **Ramp**: 10–12
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.2 – 4.0 (Inflated by target CMC, true effective CMC < 3.0)

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **High-Impact Reanimation Targets (8 cards)**: Razaketh, Archon of Cruelty, Vilis, Toxrill, Avacyn.
2. **Entomb & Discard Outlets (8 cards)**: Entomb, Buried Alive, Faithless Looting, Anje Falkenrath.
3. **Low-CMC Reanimation Spells (8 cards)**: Reanimate, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Victimize, Persist.
4. **Graveyard Protection & Value Backup (8 cards)**: Living Death, Phyrexian Reclamation, Perpetual Timepiece.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Balanced Triad**: Requires equal parts Targets, Discard/Entomb, and Reanimation Spells.
- **Graveyard Hate Countermeasures**: Run removal specifically targeting Leyline of the Void and Rest in Peace.
