# SUB-SKILL: Wheels Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 10–16
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 3–4
- **Card Draw & Wheel Spells**: 10–12 (Wheel of Fortune, Windfall, Reforge the Soul, Timetwister effects)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Fast mana & mana rocks)
- **Lands**: 36–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.8 – 3.4

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Core Wheel Spells (8 cards)**: Windfall, Wheel of Fortune, Reforge the Soul, Teferi's Puzzle Box, Whispering Madness.
2. **Wheel Punishers & Draw Drain (8 cards)**: Nekusar, Sheoldred the Apocalypse, Megrim, Liliana's Caress, Underworld Dreams.
3. **Hullbreacher / Lock Equivalents (8 cards)**: Narset Parter of Veils, Notion Thief, Orcish Bowmasters.
4. **Mana Acceleration & Spell Doublers (8 cards)**: Waste Not, Smothering Tithe, Kydele, Jeska's Will.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Wheel Punisher Setup**: Deploy wheel punishers (Sheoldred, Megrim) BEFORE casting wheel spells.
- **Mana Velocity**: Wheels refill opponent hands unless combined with punishment, waste not, or hand locks.
