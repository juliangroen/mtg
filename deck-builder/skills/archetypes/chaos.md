# SUB-SKILL: Chaos Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 15–22
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Randomized or modal interaction)
- **Board Wipes**: 4–5 (Symmetrical or disruptive clears)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Treasure generators, mana rocks)
- **Lands**: 36–38
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.2 – 3.8

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **State Inverters & Warp Effects (8 cards)**: Warp World, Scrambleverse, Knowledge Pool, Possibility Storm.
2. **Randomized Removal & Coin Flips (8 cards)**: Chaos Warp, Krark's Thumb, Game of Chaos.
3. **Asymmetric Payoffs & Coin/Dice Synergy (8 cards)**: Cards that break symmetry under chaotic conditions.
4. **Protection & Pillowfort (8 cards)**: Propaganda, Crawlspace to survive while chaos unfolds.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Break Symmetry**: True chaos without a win condition creates miserable games. Always include asymmetrical payoffs or win paths.
- **Resource Velocity**: Ensure you have high ramp density to cast 5–7 CMC chaos spells before opponents establish control.
