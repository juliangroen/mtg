# SUB-SKILL: Tribal Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 26–35 (High creature density matching tribe)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (On-theme removal preferred)
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Kindred Dominance, Asymmetric tribal clears)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Tribal draw engines like Vanquisher's Banner, Herald's Horn)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Herald's Horn, Urza's Incubator, tribal dorks)
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.5 – 3.3

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Tribal Lords & Mass Buffs (8 cards)**: Creature lords granting +1/+1 and abilities (flying, haste, deathtouch).
2. **Tribal Cost Reducers & Ramp (8 cards)**: Urza's Incubator, Herald's Horn, Sol Ring, Tribal Dorks.
3. **Tribal Draw & Velocity Engines (8 cards)**: Vanquisher's Banner, Kindred Discovery, Species Specialist.
4. **Asymmetric Tribal Board Clears & Win Cons (8 cards)**: Kindred Dominance, Patriarch's Bidding, Tribal finishers.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Tribe Density**: Maintain at least 25–30 creatures sharing the specific creature type.
- **Tribal Synergy Over Raw Power**: Prioritize cards that match the tribe over generically powerful cards.
