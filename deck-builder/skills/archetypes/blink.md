# SUB-SKILL: Blink / ETB Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 24–30 (ETB value creatures across all CMCs)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (ETB creature removal like Skyclave Apparition, Solitude)
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Asymmetric resets or bounce wipes)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (ETB draw creatures like Mulldrifter, Spirited Companion)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (ETB land ramp dorks/creatures like Wood Elves, Solemn Simulacrum)
- **Lands**: 36–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.8 – 3.4

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Repeated Blink Engines (8 cards)**: Teleportation Circle, Soulherder, Thassa Deep-Dwelling, Conjurer's Closet.
2. **Instant Mass/Single Blink (8 cards)**: Ephemerate, Ghostly Flicker, Eerie Interlude, Semester's End.
3. **ETB Value & Draw Targets (8 cards)**: Creatures that draw cards, generate tokens, or ramp on entering the battlefield.
4. **ETB Interaction & Disruption (8 cards)**: Creatures that remove permanents, counter spells, or gain control on ETB.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Engine Balance**: Maintain a 1:2 ratio of Blink Enablers to ETB Targets.
- **Instant Speed Utility**: Use mass blink spells as both value multipliers and anti-board-wipe protection.
- **Mana Efficiency**: Low-CMC blink spells (1–2 CMC) allow you to hold up protection while advancing board state.
