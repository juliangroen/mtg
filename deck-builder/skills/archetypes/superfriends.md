# SUB-SKILL: Superfriends Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Planeswalkers**: 15–22 (Planeswalker density)
- **Creatures**: 4–10 (Defensive blockers/proliferate creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 6–8 (Heavy wipes that do NOT hit planeswalkers, e.g. Supreme Verdict, Toxic Deluge)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10
- **Ramp**: 10–12
- **Lands**: 37–38
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.2 – 3.8

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Core Planeswalker Suite (8 cards)**: High-impact planeswalkers across colors.
2. **Proliferate & Loyalty Accelerators (8 cards)**: Doubling Season, Vorinclex, Deepglow Skate, Flux Channeler.
3. **Asymmetric Creature Board Clears (8 cards)**: Clears that destroy creatures while leaving planeswalkers intact.
4. **Defensive Blockers & Pillowfort (8 cards)**: Fog effects, Ghostly Prison, Silent Arbiter.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Doubling Season Rule**: Doubling Season allows many planeswalkers to ultimate the turn they enter.
- **Protecting Walkers**: Run high board-wipe density to clear combat threats against your loyalty pools.
