# SUB-SKILL: Landfall Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 18–25 (Landfall triggers & extra land enablers)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Land-based removal or instant spells)
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Asymmetric resets)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Landfall draw engines: Tatyova, Aesi, Tireless Tracker)
- **Ramp**: 12–16 (Heavy land-based ramp & extra land drops)
- **Lands**: 38–42 (Extremely high land count required)
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.0 – 3.7

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Extra Land Drop Enablers (8 cards)**: Azusa, Exploration, Oracle of Mul Daya, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove.
2. **Landfall Payoff Engines (8 cards)**: Scute Swarm, Avenger of Zendikar, Felidar Retreat, Rampaging Baloths.
3. **Graveyard Land Recursion (8 cards)**: Ramunap Excavator, Crucible of Worlds, Life from the Loam, Ancient Greenwarden.
4. **Fetch Lands & Land Tutors (8 cards)**: Crop Rotation, Harrow, Sylvan Scrying, and fetch land package.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **High Land Density**: Never run fewer than 38 lands. Fetch lands trigger landfall twice per turn.
- **Graveyard Loop Synergy**: Combine Crucible of Worlds + Fetch Lands + Extra Land Drop enablers for massive value.
