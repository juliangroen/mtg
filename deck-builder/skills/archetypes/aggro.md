# SUB-SKILL: Aggro Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 26–35 (High density of 1–3 drop threats)
- **Targeted Removal**: 5–6 (Low-CMC, efficient instant removal)
- **Board Wipes**: 1–2 (Asymmetric wipes or zero-cost protection)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Draw engines triggered on combat/damage)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Low-CMC rocks & dorks, 1–2 mana)
- **Lands**: 34–35 (Lean land count due to low curve)
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.2 – 2.8

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Early Aggressors (8 cards)**: 1–2 CMC proactive threats to establish immediate early damage.
2. **Damage Multipliers & Extra Combats (8 cards)**: Mass pump (Craterhoof, Shared Animosity), extra combat spells, or damage doublers.
3. **Evasion & Combat Enablers (8 cards)**: Unblockable grants, flying/trample enablers, and attack-trigger haste enablers.
4. **Combat-Trigger Card Draw & Protection (8 cards)**: Coastal Piracy/Toski draw effects and asymmetric protective spells (Teferi's Protection, Flawless Maneuver).

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **The 120-Life Problem**: You must deal 120 life in total across 3 opponents. Raw combat stats fail without damage multipliers, extra combats, or infect/pump.
- **Overextension & Board Clears**: Overextending into open mana gets you blown out by board wipes. Hold back threats or hold up 1-mana protection.
- **Draw Engines**: Focus heavily on draw engines that reward attacking or dealing combat damage.
