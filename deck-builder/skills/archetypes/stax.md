# SUB-SKILL: Stax Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 15–22 (Hatebears & tax creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 3–4
- **Card Draw**: 8–10
- **Ramp**: 12–14 (Heavy artifact fast mana)
- **Lands**: 36–38
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.5 – 3.2

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Rule-Setting Hatebears (8 cards)**: Thalia, Drannith Magistrate, Collector Ouphe, Grand Abolisher.
2. **Resource Denial & Taxes (8 cards)**: Winter Orb, Static Orb, Smokestack, Trinisphere.
3. **Asymmetric Symmetry Breakers (8 cards)**: Urza Lord High Artificer, Seedborn Muse, Derevi.
4. **Lock Win Conditions (8 cards)**: Karn + Mycosynth Lattice, Knowledge Pool + Teferi.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Asymmetry is Essential**: Never deploy a lock piece that hurts you as much as your opponents.
- **Fast Lock**: Deploy stax pieces early before opponents establish their mana bases.
