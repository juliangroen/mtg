# SUB-SKILL: Storm Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 4–10 (Storm enablers)
- **Targeted Removal**: 5–7 (Low CMC bounce/free spells)
- **Board Wipes**: 1–2 (Low count)
- **Card Draw & Cantrips**: 12–15 (Heavy draw & cantrip velocity)
- **Ramp & Rituals**: 12–15 (Fast mana, Dark Ritual, Cabal Ritual)
- **Lands**: 33–35 (Low land count)
- **Target Average CMC**: 1.8 – 2.5

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Rituals & Mana Generation (8 cards)**: Dark Ritual, Pyretic Ritual, Seething Song, Lotus Petal.
2. **Low-CMC Cantrips & Draw Engines (8 cards)**: Brainstorm, Gitaxian Probe, Ponder, Ad Nauseam.
3. **Storm Payoffs & Finisher Spells (8 cards)**: Grapeshot, Aetherflux Reservoir, Mind's Desire, Brain Freeze.
4. **Recursion & Past in Flames Engines (8 cards)**: Underworld Breach, Past in Flames, Yawgmoth's Will.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Ultra-Low Mana Curve**: Target average CMC must be under 2.5.
- **Storm Count Calculation**: Track available mana vs. total spells cast in turn before initiating storm off.
