# SUB-SKILL: Mill Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 12–18 (Mill triggers & defensive blockers)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Instant interaction)
- **Board Wipes**: 3–4 (Bounce & destruction wipes)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Mill-synergy draw)
- **Ramp**: 10–12
- **Lands**: 36–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.7 – 3.3

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Targeted & Mass Mill Engines (8 cards)**: Bruvac, Maddening Cacophony, Ruin Crab, Mesmeric Orb.
2. **Graveyard Punishment & Exilereflectors (8 cards)**: Dauthi Voidwalker, Leyline of the Void, Syr Konrad.
3. **Defensive Pillowfort & Blockers (8 cards)**: Propaganda, Silent Arbiter, Ensnaring Bridge.
4. **Mill Payoffs & Reanimation (8 cards)**: Animate Dead, Lazav, Breach the Multiverse.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Multiplayer Life Math for Mill**: Milling 99 cards x 3 opponents = 297 total cards. Symmetrical mill (Bruvac, Mesmeric Orb) is vital.
- **Beware Eldrazi Titan Shufflers**: Include instant graveyard exiles (Bojuka Bog, Tormod's Crypt) to prevent Kozilek/Ulamog shuffle triggers.
