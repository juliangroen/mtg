# SUB-SKILL: Midrange Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 22–28 (2-for-1 value creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 8–10 (Flexible multi-type removal)
- **Board Wipes**: 3–4 (Modal or asymmetrical clears)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Engine draw & value creatures)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Standard ramp package)
- **Lands**: 36–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.8 – 3.4

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Value Engine Creatures (8 cards)**: Beast Whisperer, Eternal Witness, Ravenous Chupacabra, Solemn Simulacrum.
2. **Threat Generation & Planeswalkers (8 cards)**: High-impact standalone threats that demand immediate removal.
3. **Flexible Interaction & Modal Spells (8 cards)**: Beast Within, Chaos Warp, Assassin's Trophy, Kolaghan's Command.
4. **Graveyard Value & Recursion (8 cards)**: Sun Titan, Meren, Victimize, Phyrexian Reclamation.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **2-for-1 Standard**: Every card should ideally generate card advantage or resolve multiple problems.
- **Adaptability**: Midrange shifts between aggressive pressure against control and defensive disruption against aggro.
