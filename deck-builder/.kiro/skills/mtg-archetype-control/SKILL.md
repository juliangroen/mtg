# SUB-SKILL: Control Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 8–15 (Utility & win condition creatures only)
- **Targeted Removal**: 12–15 (Instant-speed single target interaction & counterspells)
- **Board Wipes**: 5–7 (Asymmetric or modal resets)
- **Card Draw**: 12–14 (Sustained draw engines & instant-speed refills)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Mana rocks & land ramp, heavy on untap effects)
- **Lands**: 37–39 (High land count to ensure hitting every land drop)
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.0 – 3.6

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Counterspell Suite (8 cards)**: Counterspell, Mana Drain, Dovin's Veto, Fierce Guardianship.
2. **Instant Spot Removal (8 cards)**: Swords to Plowshares, Cyclonic Rift, Beast Within, Anguished Unmaking.
3. **Asymmetric Wipes & Resets (8 cards)**: Farewell, Supreme Verdict, Toxic Deluge, Damnation.
4. **Late-Game Finishers & Value Engines (8 cards)**: Rhystic Study, Smothering Tithe, Torrential Gearhulk, Consecrated Sphinx.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Threat Assessment**: In 4-player Commander, trading 1-for-1 puts you behind two players. Save removal for win threats or engine pieces.
- **Land Drop Priority**: Never miss a land drop through turn 8. High land count (37–39) is non-negotiable.
- **Asymmetric Clears**: Favor wipes that leave your commander or engines intact.
