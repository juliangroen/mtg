# SUB-SKILL: Tokens Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 18–25 (Token generators & payoff creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8
- **Board Wipes**: 2–3 (Asymmetric or token-sparing wipes)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Draw on creature entry/death, e.g., Shamanic Revelation, Skullclamp)
- **Ramp**: 10–12
- **Lands**: 35–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.6 – 3.2

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Token Generators (8 cards)**: Scute Swarm, Secure the Wastes, Avenger of Zendikar, Parallel Lives.
2. **Token Doublers & Parallel Generators (8 cards)**: Parallel Lives, Anointed Procession, Doubling Season, Adrix and Nev.
3. **Mass Anthems & Overrun Finishers (8 cards)**: Craterhoof Behemoth, Triumph of the Hordes, Beastmaster Ascension.
4. **Sacrifice & Death Payoffs / Protection (8 cards)**: Skullclamp, Teferi's Protection, Idol of Oblivion.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Go-Wide Finisher**: Always include 3+ mass pump/overrun effects to convert wide token boards into immediate lethal damage.
- **Wipe Protection**: Board wipes wipe out token armies completely; hold up instant protection or draw engines.
