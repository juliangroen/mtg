# SUB-SKILL: Enchantress Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 10–18 (Enchantress engines & synergy creatures)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Enchantment-based removal like Oblivion Ring, Song of the Dryads)
- **Board Wipes**: 3–4 (Enchantment-based or asymmetric resets)
- **Card Draw**: 8–10 (Enchantress draw engines: Sythis, Argothian Enchantress, Mesa Enchantress)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Land aura ramp like Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Fertile Ground)
- **Lands**: 36–37
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.7 – 3.3

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Enchantress Draw Engines (8 cards)**: Sythis, Enchantress's Presence, Satyr Enchanter, Verduran Enchantress.
2. **Enchantment Ramp & Cost Reducers (8 cards)**: Wild Growth, Utopia Sprawl, Starfield Mystic, Jorael's Favor.
3. **Pillowfort & Defensive Auras (8 cards)**: Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety, Sterling Grove, Privilege.
4. **Finisher Win Conditions (8 cards)**: Sigil of the Empty Throne, Destiny Spinner, Finale of Devastation, Luminarch Ascension.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Enchantment Critical Mass**: Aim for 30+ total enchantments in the 99 so draw engines trigger consistently.
- **Protection Priority**: Protect your draw engines with Sterling Grove or Privileged Position early.
