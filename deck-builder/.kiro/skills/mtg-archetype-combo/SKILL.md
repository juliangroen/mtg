# SUB-SKILL: Combo Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: Varies (Low to Moderate, focused on combo pieces)
- **Targeted Removal**: 6–8 (Cheap instant-speed interaction)
- **Board Wipes**: 1–3 (Low count, highly efficient or bounce)
- **Card Draw & Cantrips**: 10–12 (Heavy velocity & filtering)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Fast mana, rituals, 2-CMC rocks)
- **Lands**: 34–36 (Lean mana base)
- **Target Average CMC**: 2.2 – 2.9

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Primary Combo Package (8 cards)**: Essential 2–3 card infinite/lethal win conditions (e.g., Thoracle/Consultation, Heliod/Ballista).
2. **Tutors & Transmute Enablers (8 cards)**: Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Worldly Tutor.
3. **Protection & Counter-Grip (8 cards)**: Silence, Fierce Guardianship, Veil of Summer, Deflecting Swat to protect the combo turn.
4. **Recursion & Piece Backup (8 cards)**: Noxious Revival, Underworld Breach, Regrowth to recover countered/milled pieces.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Redundancy Over Fragility**: Prefer combo packages where pieces share functional overlap or commander synergy.
- **Protect the Window**: Never attempt to deploy a combo without counter/protection backup unless forced.
- **Tutor Efficiency**: Use low-CMC tutors to lower effective combo casting cost.
