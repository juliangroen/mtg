# SUB-SKILL: Group Hug Commander Archetype

## 1. Ratio Overrides vs. Baseline
- **Creatures**: 12–18
- **Targeted Removal**: 8–10 (Instant speed to stop sudden win attempts)
- **Board Wipes**: 4–5 (Resets to keep runaway players in check)
- **Card Draw**: 10–12 (Symmetrical draw engines: Howling Mine, Kami of the Crescent Moon)
- **Ramp**: 10–12 (Symmetrical mana acceleration: Dictate of Karametra, Tempt with Discovery)
- **Lands**: 37–38
- **Target Average CMC**: 3.0 – 3.6

## 2. Recommended 8x8 Custom Categories (32 cards)
1. **Symmetrical Resource Acceleration (8 cards)**: Howling Mine, Heartbeat of Spring, Ghirapur Orrery.
2. **Pillowfort & Threat Redirection (8 cards)**: Propaganda, Ghostly Prison, Gluntch, Gwafa Hazid.
3. **Sudden Asymmetric Win Conditions (8 cards)**: Insurrection, Approach of the Second Sun, Forced Fruition.
4. **Targeted Kingmaker Control (8 cards)**: Instant-speed answers to neutralize players trying to break symmetry.

## 3. Strategic Rules & Traps
- **Weaponized Generosity**: Giving resources without a clear asymmetric plan guarantees the fastest deck wins. Always have a hidden win path.
- **Break Symmetry**: Ensure your deck benefits more from extra mana/cards than your opponents.
